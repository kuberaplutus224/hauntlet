# Product

**Hauntlet** is a defensive security range for AI agents. A developer points their
own agent (browser, coding, or MCP client) at it and receives an autopsy report
of how the agent behaved against known, published classes of hostile content,
followed by generated Kiro guardrails that close the gaps.

- **Users:** developers and teams shipping agents who need to test their own
  harness, tools and permissions — not just the underlying model.
- **Why it exists:** vendors' own 2026 papers say injection via read content is
  unsolved and model-level defences are insufficient, yet their published numbers
  are model-level. The missing instrument measures a *real deployment*.
- **Not:** an attack tool, a model jailbreak service, or a leaderboard that
  publishes named vendors' failures without disclosure.
- **Shape:** one hosted range + a one-command local runner, scorecard, and the
  Exorcist fix loop. OSI-licensed, public repo (Kiroween requirement).
